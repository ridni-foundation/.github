## Ridni Foundation

Ridni is a Ukrainian charitable foundation whose mission is to overcome
orphanhood, supporting families and children.

Our website, [ridni.org.ua](https://ridni.org.ua/en/), is where most of our
individual donors find us, and we build the tools behind it ourselves. When a
tool is useful beyond our own site, we publish it here under an open-source
licence.

### Projects

- [connect-wcdp-elementor](https://github.com/ridni-foundation/connect-wcdp-elementor) —
  Elementor widgets and dynamic tags for Donation Platform for WooCommerce:
  donation progress, donor walls and campaign figures, fully styleable, with
  live figures on cached pages.

### Get involved

- Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue in the project's repository.
- Security reports: use "Report a vulnerability" in the repository, or write to
  community@ridni.org.ua.
- Support our work: [donate](https://ridni.org.ua/en/donation/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ridni-foundation-profile&utm_content=profile-donate).
